### Finding 1
- Severity: low
- Category: workflow
- Summary: `buildReviewInstruction` tells the agent to check the "Static Analysis Findings (Fallow) block above" even when `staticAnalysisPrompt` is empty and no such block was prepended.
- Evidence:
  - `buildReviewInstruction` (helpers.ts) builds requirement 11 unconditionally: "If the Static Analysis Findings (Fallow) block above contains issues..."
  - When Fallow is unavailable, `staticAnalysisPrompt` is empty and no Fallow block is prepended to the instruction.
  - The agent may search for a non-existent block or be confused about what to copy.
- Recommendation: Make requirement 11 conditional. When `staticAnalysisPrompt` is non-empty, instruct copying verbatim findings. When empty, instruct writing `- No Fallow findings.` directly without referencing a missing block.
- Rule candidate: When an instruction references content that is conditionally prepended, the guidance about that content must also be conditional.
