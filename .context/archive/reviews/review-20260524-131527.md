### Finding 2
- Severity: low
- Category: simplification
- Summary: Review-remediation state transitions are now split between in-memory flags and direct markdown rewrites, but that contract is encoded only implicitly in scattered helpers.
- Evidence: `processCompleteStoryReviewCloseout(...)` and the manual-review `turn_end` branch both set `reviewCloseoutReady: false` and then call `setReviewInProgressForRemediation(...)`. The helper rewrites `**Status:**` and `**Completed:**` by regex in-place, while closeout eligibility is still partially derived by `parseReviewFrontmatter(...)` and partially by `reviewCloseoutReady`. - Recommendation: Extract a small shared transition helper that moves a review from “complete and waiting for closeout” back to “in-progress for remediation” in one call, updating both persisted frontmatter and in-memory state together.
