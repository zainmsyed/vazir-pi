## Findings
### Finding 1
- Severity: medium
- Category: bug
- Summary: `finalizeReviewFileForCloseout()` can mark UI complete-story reviews complete even when the UI-only design checklist is still unchecked.
- Evidence: The new helper only inspects the `## Checklist` section via `reviewChecklistLooksComplete()` in `.pi/extensions/vazir-context/complete-story.ts`. UI review templates also include a separate `## Design Compliance (UI stories only)` checklist in `.pi/extensions/vazir-context/helpers.ts`, but those unchecked items are ignored by the auto-finalization gate. A structurally “almost done” UI review can therefore flip to `**Status:** complete` and unlock closeout choices without the reviewer ever completing the required design checks.
- Recommendation: Extend the closeout-finalization gate to validate every relevant checklist block for the review template, including `## Design Compliance (UI stories only)` when present, and add a regression scenario proving unchecked UI design items keep the review in progress.
- Rule candidate: When auto-finalizing a workflow document from checklist state, validate all checklist sections that the template requires, not just the primary checklist block.
